Privacy policy

Olympiad Atlas is an independent maths practice website maintained by the owner of the 2319bli/olympiad-atlas project. This policy covers the GitHub Pages website and its Cloudflare account service.

For privacy questions, corrections or account deletion, contact 2319bli@gmail.com.

Information Atlas uses

InformationPurpose and storage
Google account identifier, name and email addressWhen you choose Google sign-in, Atlas verifies your identity and stores these details in its Cloudflare D1 database to identify your account and show who is signed in.
Saved mock papers, bookmarked problem IDs and practice statusGuest data stays in your browser. Signed-in data is stored in your browser and synced to Cloudflare D1 so you can use it on another device. Guest data is only copied into your account when you use the import control.
Session and sign-in security dataYour browser stores an Atlas session token. The server stores its hash and expiry, and temporary sign-in challenges, to authenticate requests and prevent replay.
Support correspondenceIf you email the maintainer, the information you provide is used to respond to your request.

Google sign-in

Google handles your Google password; Atlas does not receive it. Atlas uses basic identity information only, with no access to your Gmail messages, Drive files, contacts or calendar. Google may include a profile image in its sign-in response; Atlas does not store that image in its account database.

Google user data is used to provide sign-in and account features. It is not sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models. Signing in does not send your saved papers or practice marks to an AI service.

Hosting and other services

GitHub Pages serves the website. Cloudflare Workers and D1 handle account requests and storage. Google provides sign-in and the fonts used by the main app. These providers receive network information such as IP addresses and request details when your browser connects to them, and may process data on infrastructure outside your country.

Atlas has no advertising or analytics scripts of its own. Account data is not published in the GitHub source repository or shared with other Atlas users. The maintainer can access server records to operate the service and handle support requests. Hosting providers process data as part of delivering their services.

See the GitHub privacy statement, Cloudflare privacy policy and Google privacy policy. Competition source links take you to independent websites with their own policies.

Browser storage and retention

Atlas uses local browser storage for guest practice, signed-in account caches, pending sync changes and its session token. Clearing site data removes those local copies; it does not delete synced server records. Signing out revokes the current session, but does not erase cached practice data from the device.

Atlas sessions expire after 30 days. Sign-in challenges expire after 10 minutes; expired security records are cleaned up when new sign-in challenges are issued. Accounts, papers and progress have no automatic expiry and remain stored until removed by the maintainer. Provider backups or operational logs may persist under the provider's retention arrangements.

Your choices and requests

You can browse and practise without signing in. Use the export controls to download your papers and practice data. You can change practice marks in the app, sign out, or remove Atlas's Google connection through your Google Account connections. Removing the Google connection does not by itself delete Atlas data.

There is currently no self-service account deletion button. To request access, correction or deletion of server-held data, email 2319bli@gmail.com from the account concerned. Account ownership may need to be verified before a request is carried out. Never send a password or session token. For local copies, also clear Atlas site data on each device after exporting anything you want to keep.
