Terms of use

Olympiad Atlas is a free, independent resource for exploring olympiad problems, generating practice papers and tracking your practice. It is maintained by the owner of the 2319bli/olympiad-atlas project. Contact: 2319bli@gmail.com.

Educational use

Atlas is not an official UKMT or competition-organiser service. Generated mock papers are practice material, not official competition papers. Difficulty ratings are approximate AI assessments, not official ratings or measured probabilities of solving a problem. Check original sources when accuracy or competition rules matter.

Problem sources and rights

Original problem authors and competition organisers retain their rights. Source links and attribution identify the material used. Using Atlas or downloading a generated PDF does not transfer those rights or give blanket permission to republish third-party material. Follow each source's licence and any applicable permissions. The Sources & methodology section in Atlas gives further attribution.

Accounts and responsible use

Google sign-in is optional and enables cloud storage for your papers and progress. Use an account you are authorised to use, import only data that belongs to you, and sign out on shared devices. Do not attempt to access other users' data, bypass security controls, interfere with the service or overload its infrastructure.

Your account data is handled as described in the privacy policy. Do not include passwords, sensitive personal information or another person's private data in paper titles or imported files.

Leaderboards

Leaderboards use self-reported solved marks. Each distinct bank question contributes once; attempts do not add points. The weighted ranking adds 1 plus the current Atlas difficulty for each solved question. Corrected ratings, duplicate merges and corrected practice marks may change totals. Use accurate marks and an appropriate public display name; do not impersonate another person. Participation is optional and can be turned off on the Leaderboard page.

Availability and backups

Atlas currently has no charges or subscriptions. It depends on third-party hosting and may experience interruptions, usage limits or changes to features. Content can contain errors. Keep exported backups of papers and practice data that matter to you; syncing is not a substitute for your own backup.
